Welcome to Green Hills Coffee Plantation

Discover the beauty and taste of Kenyan coffee.

Green Hills Coffee Plantation is a beautiful coffee farm surrounded by green hills and a peaceful natural environment.The Plantation has rows of coffee trees growing on the fertile land,creating an attractive view.The farmers work hard to maintain the coffee plants by removing weeds,applying fertilizers, and controllling pests and diseases.When the coffee berries ripen,they carefully harvested and taken for processing.The plantation provides employment opportunities to local people and contribute to the economic development of the surrounding community.Green hills coffee plantation is an important place for coffee production and showcases the beauty of nature and the hardwork of farmers.
